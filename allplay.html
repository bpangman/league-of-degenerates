All-Play Standings

Every team's record if it had played every other score posted that week, every week. This is the fraud detector. Sorted by all-play record, not the standings you actually got.

How to read this table: "All-Play" is the record each team would have if it played every other team's score every single week, all season. "Exp. W" converts that all-play winning percentage into an expected win total over the real 14-week schedule. "Luck" is actual wins minus expected wins - positive means the schedule was kind to that team, negative means it was not. This is the table that proves Tony Loff's 11-3 was a product of the schedule, and that Team Barbie got robbed.
