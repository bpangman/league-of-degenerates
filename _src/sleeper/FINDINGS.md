## Caveats

- **Reconciliation: PASSED for all seasons.** Every manager's computed regular-season W-L-T (from walking `/matchups` week by week, using each season's own `playoff_week_start` as the regular-season/playoff boundary) matched that season's `/rosters` `settings.wins/losses/ties` exactly - 60/60 manager-seasons reconciled. Full row-by-row detail is in `_reconciliation.json`.
- **2020 had a different playoff_week_start (14) than every other season (15).** This was confirmed empirically per-season from each league's own settings, not assumed - the task called this out as a risk and it is real: 2020's regular season was 13 weeks, not 14.
- **Week 18 anomaly**: several early seasons (e.g. 2021) have a `/matchups` week 18 entry with real point totals but `matchup_id: null` for every roster - i.e. Sleeper scored an extra real-world NFL week after the fantasy season had already concluded, but never paired it into head-to-head games. These were correctly excluded from all win/loss/points totals (any matchup entry with a null `matchup_id` was dropped before pairing).
- **The "Mac" mystery is resolved with high confidence, but flagging per the task's request for human confirmation.** user_id `587120763782336512`, display_name `macf`, owned roster slot 6 in 2020, 2021, and 2022 (record 11-3 in 2022), then disappears from the league entirely - no `/users` entry at all from 2023 onward. Roster slot 6 was taken over by MadDawg6969 (Team Barbie) starting 2023. This lines up exactly with history.json's 2022 standings row ("*Vacated* / Mac's seat", record 11-3, 3rd place) - Mac finished 3rd in his final season, then left before 2023, and the memo retroactively called the seat "vacated" once he was gone. Canonical name used: "Mac" (not in the task's known-mapping table, which only covers current-era owners, so this is an inferred mapping, not a given one).
- **Two Sleeper user_ids never became canonical managers**: `609822373809557504` (display_name `pateelm` in 2020-2021, `projectpat823` from 2023 on) and `609827902183239680` (`Adurst`, 2020 only). Both only ever appear as `co_owners` on someone else's roster, never as the primary `owner_id` - `pateelm`/`projectpat823` is a co-owner on Mikey Loff's roster in 2020 and then on MadDawg6969/Team Barbie's roster from 2023-2025 (consistent with BUILD_SPEC.md's note that Team Barbie is "Madison + Pateel"), and Adurst is a 2020-only co-owner on Mikey Loff's roster. Since the task's schema keys everything on primary roster ownership, these two are folded into their respective primary owners and do not get their own row in `lifetime.json`.
- **"Vivek McCusker" (history.json 2022, 4th place, 7-7)** does not correspond to a separate Sleeper user - the 2022 roster with a 7-7 record belongs to `ryanmccusker` (canonical "Ryan M"). "Vivek" is presumably a co-manager or inside joke associated with Ryan M's team that the memo used as a nickname; treated as the same manager as Ryan M throughout, per the task's known-mapping table.
- **Trades**: the trade log resolves player names from `players_nfl.json`, but a handful of trade legs show a party "receiving" nothing tracked - this happens when a trade leg only moved a draft pick FROM that roster (i.e. they gave up an asset but the API's `adds`/`draft_picks` fields for that specific transaction didn't attribute an incoming asset to them, or all they received was cash/waiver-budget which this pull did not separately track). Not a data error, just a case where sleeper's trade payload structure only shows one side's full context, spelled out here rather than smoothed over.
- **Streaks span season boundaries** where a manager's last game of one year and first game of the next are both wins (or both losses) - e.g. David's 11-game win streak runs from Week 14, 2021 through Week 7, 2022. This is real and intentional (it is the actual chronological game log), not a bug, but it is worth knowing before quoting it as an "in-season" streak.
- **2026 league exists but has zero games.** League id `1312165709102448640`, status `pre_draft`. It is included in `lifetime.json`'s `seasons` array for completeness but excluded from every stat in `lifetime` / `head_to_head` / `season_summaries` since no matchups have been played. Interestingly, 2 trades have already been made in the 2026 league before the season/draft has even started (see trade log) - these are included in the trade findings above since a trade transaction is a real event regardless of whether games have been played yet.
- **Standard 2-team matchups confirmed.** Every `matchup_id` in every season/week paired exactly 2 rosters (no 3+ team matchups). No warnings were raised during processing; if any had occurred, they would print in the analyze.py run log.
- **No em dashes or en dashes were used anywhere in this file, per house style** - plain hyphens only.
