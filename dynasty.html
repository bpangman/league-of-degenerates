The Dynasty Board

Six years of trades, extensions, and busts, pulled from the contract spreadsheet and computed in storylines.md.

How to read a contract number: a number is the years of team control remaining beyond the listed season. 0 means the listed season is the last guaranteed year. No number at all means the player is on expiring rights only, no contract security going into next year's draft pool.

The Iron Men (2020 - 2026, never moved since the origin draft)

16 players have sat on the same roster every single season since the league's origin 2020 draft, never traded or cut. Three more players - Lamar Jackson, Josh Allen, and Travis Kelce - have been locked onto their current roster just as long, but their streak starts a few weeks later than the rest: they were acquired in the flurry of trades that opened that first 2020 season, before Week 1 was ever played, and haven't moved since. Eight of the ten current owners have a cornerstone dating back to that opening window. Hannah alone owns seven of the 16 true never-movers, nearly half of the league's total loyalty pool, all on one roster.

The Hannah Cliff
All seven of Hannah's 2020-vintage never-moved cornerstones are expiring at once: Watson, McCaffery, Jonathan Taylor, Pollard, Sutton, CeeDee Lamb, Justin Jefferson. Her only long-term piece is Ashton Jeanty. The most stable roster in league history is about to get detonated in a single draft.
The Bare Cupboard
David has zero players locked up 3+ years, and only 2 total contract years locked in across a 19-man roster, the lowest total in the league by a factor of 3. His stockpile rank has finished dead last (10th) three straight seasons: 2024, 2025, 2026.
The Best-Positioned
Anthony (Tony Loff) has finished #1 in the league in total locked-in contract years every season from 2021 through 2026, six years running. He has 4 players locked 3+ years and 18 total locked-in years, roughly 9x David's total.

Busts

Multi-year contracts that vanished from every roster in the league within a season or two of being signed.

5-yr deal, 2021

Trey Lance - Matt

Signed for 5 years in 2021. Liquidated down to 1 remaining year by 2023, gone from the entire league by then. Two seasons of return on a five-year bet. The Commissioner's own memo calls it the worst allocation of capital in league history.

5-yr deal, 2020

Kenny Golladay - Levon

Signed for 5 years in 2020. On Levon's roster through 2022, then formally liquidated for 2 years on Singletary instead. Gone from the league after 2022, 3 of 5 committed years delivered.

4-yr + 3-yr deals

Bryce Young - Hannah

Given 4 years in 2023, then a fresh 3-year re-sign in 2024. Gone from the entire league by the end of 2024. Two commitments, zero payoff.

6-yr deal, 2020

Kyler Murray - Levon then Anthony

Levon spent a 6-year commitment on him in 2020, traded him away after one season. Anthony then spent a fresh 5-year commitment on the return and still lost him a year before that term was up. Two different owners, two different contracts, same player, neither deal paid off in full.

Trading Habits

Anthony (Tony Loff): the trading addict

Party to roughly 20 of the 37 verbatim trades logged across 7 seasons, appearing in every single season's trade log without exception (5 separate deals in the 2023 tab alone). Also #1 in locked-in contract years for six straight years. He upgrades and re-ups constantly rather than churning.

Hannah: the opposite of Anthony

Lowest or second-lowest season-to-season roster turnover almost every year. In 2025 to 2026 she retained 21 of her 22 players, just 5 percent turnover. That extreme loyalty is exactly what set up the 2026 Cliff above: her whole core expires at once because she never diversified when.

David: the sharpest collapse

Total locked-in contract years fell from 33 in 2020 (8th in the league) to 13 in 2023 to just 2 by 2026, three consecutive last-place finishes in the league's contract-stockpile ranking. No other owner shows a decline this steep or this sustained.

League-wide trend

Everyone got more conservative about long-term deals over time. Total locked-in contract years league-wide fell from 328 in 2020 to just 102 in 2026, though part of that is simply that the 2026 season is still in progress and most incoming rookies have not been extended yet.
