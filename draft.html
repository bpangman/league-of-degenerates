Who Actually Holds Each Pick

Every manager's finish in 2025 earned them a slot in the draft order. Some of those slots have since been traded away. "Earned" means the pick still belongs to the manager whose finish created it. "Acquired" means it moved.

Back to Back to Back
Ryan M holds picks 1.07, 1.08, and 1.09 - three of the top nine picks in the entire draft, in a row. He kept his own earned pick (1.07) and acquired both David's (1.08) and Tony Loff's (1.09).
Zero First-Rounders
Tony Loff and BlakeBro both hold zero picks in Round 1. Tony Loff traded his earned first (1.09) to Ryan M. BlakeBro traded his earned first (1.04) to David in the Devon Achane deal. Neither acquired a first-round pick in return.

Gray "Earned" badge = still held by the manager whose 2025 finish created the slot. Red "Acquired" badge = the pick moved via trade. Source: sleeper/draft2026.json, cross-checked field by field.

A correction to keep this page honest: earlier planning notes for this site claimed Tony Loff and David would both have zero first-round picks. That is not what the draft data actually shows. David traded away his own earned pick (1.08) to Ryan M, but separately acquired BlakeBro's earned pick (1.04) in the trade that sent Devon Achane to David. Those two moves net out to David holding exactly one first-round pick, 1.04, not zero. The two managers who genuinely hold zero first-round picks are Tony Loff and BlakeBro. This page reflects the real data in sleeper/draft2026.json, not the earlier claim.
