Trading Habits

Anthony (Tony Loff): the trading addict

Party to roughly 20 of the 37 verbatim trades logged across 7 seasons, appearing in every single season's trade log without exception (5 separate deals in the 2023 tab alone). Also #1 in locked-in contract years for six straight years. He upgrades and re-ups constantly rather than churning.

Hannah: the opposite of Anthony

Lowest or second-lowest season-to-season roster turnover almost every year. In 2025 to 2026 she retained 21 of her 22 players, just 5 percent turnover. That extreme loyalty is exactly what set up the 2026 Cliff above: her whole core expires at once because she never diversified when.

David: the sharpest collapse

League-wide trend

Everyone got more conservative about long-term deals over time. Total locked-in contract years league-wide fell from 328 in 2020 to just 102 in 2026, though part of that is simply that the 2026 season is still in progress and most incoming rookies have not been extended yet.
